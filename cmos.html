Introduction

What is CMOS?

CMOS was founded by Abdur-Rahmaan Archer and grew out of years of work on Qatar's biggest stages, including the FIFA World Cup 2022, WSJ Tech Live and the T100 Triathlon World Tour.

That experience shapes everything CMOS does: clear planning, calm execution, and creative work that looks as good on a phone screen as it does on a stage.

We work in English and Arabic with businesses, brands and event organisers across Qatar and the region.
