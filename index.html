About

Built on the court. Proven on the world stage.

I'm a Canadian-Moroccan creative and operations professional based in Doha. I played professional basketball under the Qatar Basketball Federation for Al Ahli Sports Club, then started my own youth coaching programme in 2019.

Since then I've worked behind the scenes at some of the region's biggest events, from the FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022 to WSJ Tech Live, the T100 Triathlon World Tour, the West Asia Rugby 7s and the opening of the 3-2-1 Qatar Olympic & Sports Museum. I've also worked as a personal assistant to a CEO and built partnerships with 30+ organisations.

Trillonheir is where all of it comes together. I speak English fluently and Arabic at a professional level.
