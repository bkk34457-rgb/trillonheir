SWISH Academy.

Nothin' but net. SWISH is the destination for developing basketball skills and refining athletic talent, with training programs built for players of all ages and levels, from beginners to professionals.

Our mission

  • Teach the fundamentals of basketball and its rules.
  • Provide high-quality training led by certified, experienced coaches in a safe, supportive environment.
  • Build essential skills: shooting, passing, defence and physical fitness.
  • Encourage teamwork, discipline and sportsmanship.
  • Organise events and competitions where players test their skills at every level.
