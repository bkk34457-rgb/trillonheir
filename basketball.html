Academy & training

The academy.

A structured training program for young players who want to get better every week. Sessions are built around the basics that matter: footwork, ball handling, shooting form, defence and basketball IQ.

Every player gets a coach who has played at the professional level and knows what it takes to grow, from first dribble to competitive team play.

  • Age-grouped sessions, from 5 years old upward
  • Small groups so every player gets attention
  • Progress tracked season to season
