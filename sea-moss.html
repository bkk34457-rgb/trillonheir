What is sea moss?

Sea moss is a seaweed that has been part of traditional diets for generations, especially in the Caribbean.

Blended into a smooth gel, it's easy to add to smoothies, juices, tea, oats or desserts.

Ours is wild-harvested and organic, sourced from South America and made into gels for delivery across Qatar.
