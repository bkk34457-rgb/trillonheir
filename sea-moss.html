What is sea moss?

Sea moss is a red seaweed harvested from the ocean. It's been part of traditional diets for generations, especially in the Caribbean and Ireland.

It naturally contains minerals like iodine, calcium, potassium and magnesium. Once cleaned, soaked and blended, it becomes a smooth gel you can add to smoothies, juices, tea, oats or desserts.

Our sea moss is cleaned by hand and blended fresh, with no fillers or preservatives.
